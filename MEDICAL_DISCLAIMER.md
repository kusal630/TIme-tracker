# Medical Disclaimer

## Important Notice

**DailyTrack is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.**

## Educational Purpose Only

The information provided by DailyTrack, including but not limited to:

- Health indicators and body system cards
- Nutrition analysis and recommendations
- Sleep and recovery assessments
- Growth score components
- Activity and exercise tracking insights

...is for **educational and informational purposes only**. This information is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Not a Diagnosis

DailyTrack does **not** diagnose any medical condition, disease, or disorder. The app provides lifestyle indicators and general observations based on the data you enter.

## No Treatment Recommendations

DailyTrack does **not** provide treatment recommendations. Any suggestions provided by the app are general lifestyle observations, not medical prescriptions.

## Seek Professional Advice

Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in DailyTrack.

## Emergency Situations

If you experience severe symptoms such as:

- Severe chest pain
- Severe shortness of breath
- Fainting or loss of consciousness
- Signs of severe allergic reaction
- Signs of severe dehydration
- Thoughts of self-harm

**Seek immediate emergency medical attention.** DailyTrack is not designed for emergency situations.

While DailyTrack strives to provide accurate information, the app makes no representations or warranties about the accuracy, reliability, completeness, or timeliness of any information provided.

## Individual Variability

Health indicators and recommendations in DailyTrack are based on general guidelines and may not be appropriate for all individuals. Factors such as age, sex, medical conditions, medications, and individual variability can significantly affect health outcomes.

## Your Responsibility

You are responsible for your own health decisions. The use of DailyTrack does not create a doctor-patient relationship between you and the app developers.
