# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01

## Overview

DailyTrack is an offline-first, privacy-first life tracking application. All personal data stays on your device.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.** DailyTrack operates entirely on your device. There is:

- No cloud storage
- No analytics
- No telemetry
- No crash reporting
- No advertising
- No user accounts
- No data upload to any server

## Data Storage

All data you enter into DailyTrack is stored locally on your Android device using the Room database. This data includes:

- Time tracking sessions
- Food and drink logs
- Sleep records
- Exercise logs
- Body metrics
- Mood check-ins
- Journal entries
- Life events
- App settings

## Data Sharing

DailyTrack does not share your data with any third parties. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

## Optional Features

Some optional features may require additional permissions:

- **Notifications**: Used for timer alerts and optional reminders
- **Camera**: Used for optional barcode scanning and food photos (disabled by default)
- **Health Connect**: Optional integration for importing health data (disabled by default)

These features are disabled by default and require your explicit consent to enable.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through Settings > Delete All Data. This action is irreversible.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app's settings.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the project repository.
