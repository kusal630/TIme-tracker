Soul Track is an offline-first, privacy-first life tracking app that helps you understand and improve your daily life without sending any data to the cloud.

TRACK YOUR WHOLE LIFE
• Time Tracking — Active timer, manual logging, session management
• Productivity Analysis — See how much time is productive, wasted, or spent learning
• Growth Score — A transparent daily score based on your habits, health, and productivity
• Comfort Zone Detection — Knows when you are stuck doing the same things
• Food and Nutrition — Log meals with full macro and micronutrient tracking
• Hydration — Water intake tracking with configurable daily goals
• Sleep and Recovery — Sleep debt calculation with recovery warnings
• Exercise and Movement — Log workouts and get inactivity alerts
• Mood and Journal — Quick mood check-ins and daily reflections
• Life Events — Timeline of milestones and important moments
• Routine Detection — Finds repetitive patterns in your daily life

INSIGHTS AND ANALYTICS
• Personalized warnings and suggestions based on your data
• Charts and trend analysis across all tracked areas
• Export and Import — Backup and restore your data as JSON or CSV

PRIVACY FIRST
• All data stays on your device always
• No cloud accounts required
• No ads, no telemetry, no analytics
• No proprietary dependencies
• Open source — inspect every line of code

Your life data belongs to you. No servers, no corporations, no compromises.
