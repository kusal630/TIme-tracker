name: Soul Track
com.name: io.github.dailytrack
summary: Offline-first life tracking with timer, pomodoro, todos, and growth insights.
description: |
  Soul Track is a privacy-first life tracking app that runs entirely
  on your device. No internet required, no accounts needed, no data
  leaves your phone.

  Features:
  - Time tracking with category-based sessions
  - Pomodoro timer with customizable work/break durations
  - Todo list with priorities, deadlines, and subtasks
  - Growth score calculation across learning, productivity, exercise,
    sleep, social, novelty, and consistency
  - Insights with charts and trend analysis
  - Home screen widgets for quick access
  - Local Syncthing sync between devices (optional)
  - Dark theme with customizable colors

  All data stays on your device. Sync is optional and uses Syncthing
  for encrypted local network transfer.
